      The story

      From refuge to riddim

      Paolos TK blends the rhythms of Ethiopia and Eritrea with the heart of Jamaican reggae. His journey, from seeking refuge to building a new life in America, runs through every song.

      He released his debut album, Contagious Love, in 1991. In the early '90s he was a founding member of Zionsoundz in Santa Cruz, California, alongside Miguel Migs, who went on to become an internationally known house music producer and DJ. The band recorded its album in 1993.

      Santa Cruz · early '90s
      Zionsoundz × Miguel Migs

      Before he became one of house music's best-known producers and DJs, Miguel Migs played alongside Paolos TK in the roots reggae band Zionsoundz. Hear the band's top track, "Babylon by the River".

      His band over the years has included bassist Henry Sarmiento, who went on to found Sonic Vista Studios in Ibiza, Spain, and has engineered for artists including David Guetta and Lady Gaga.

      Solo albums Liberty (1999) and Wounded Bear (2004) followed, along with Bella (2016), three songs with Al Pancho, "Bella," "One for Me" and "Big Ganja Spliff" featuring Ras Elias, released on Zion Soundz Records and distributed by VP Records (VPAL), one of the world's leading reggae labels. Now based in Eugene, Oregon, Paolos TK is bringing new music, international collaborations and live shows to a new generation of roots fans.
